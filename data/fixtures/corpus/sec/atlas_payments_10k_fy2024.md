## Item 1. Business

Atlas Payments Holdings plc provides merchant acquiring, payment gateway and issuer processing services to approximately 740,000 merchant locations across 31 countries. The Company processed 48.2 billion transactions with a total payment volume of 1.84 trillion dollars in 2024. Revenue is earned principally as a percentage of payment volume, net of interchange and scheme fees.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

We are exposed to merchant credit risk through chargebacks. When a merchant ceases trading before delivering goods, we are obliged to fund cardholder refunds and may be unable to recover them. Chargeback losses net of recoveries were 94.6 million dollars in 2024, of which 38.1 million dollars related to a single travel sector merchant that entered administration in the third quarter.

We operate in a heavily regulated environment and hold electronic money and payment institution licences in multiple jurisdictions. Regulatory capital requirements and safeguarding obligations restrict the use of a portion of our cash. Safeguarded client funds of 3,110 million dollars are held in segregated accounts and are not available for general corporate purposes.

Our fraud detection systems must adapt continuously. Card-not-present fraud attempts rose 23 percent by count in 2024 while the value of fraud losses we absorbed fell 8 percent, reflecting model improvements deployed in the first half. A material degradation in detection performance would increase losses and could trigger scheme monitoring programmes.

Interchange and scheme fee regulation directly affects our pricing. Regulatory caps introduced in two of our European markets during 2024 reduced net revenue by an estimated 41 million dollars on an annualised basis.

Concentration in our gateway infrastructure is a risk. Approximately 63 percent of authorisation traffic is routed through two data centres operating in an active-active configuration. We completed a failover exercise in October 2024 with a measured recovery time of 4 minutes 20 seconds.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Net revenue for 2024 was 3,418.2 million dollars, an increase of 11.6 percent from 3,062.8 million dollars in 2023. Growth was driven by a 9 percent increase in total payment volume and a 14 percent increase in value-added service attachment, partially offset by regulatory fee caps in Europe.

Adjusted EBITDA was 1,428.9 million dollars, a margin of 41.8 percent, compared with 1,247.3 million dollars, a margin of 40.7 percent, in 2023. Net income was 612.4 million dollars, or 1.94 dollars per diluted share.

Capital expenditure was 284.1 million dollars, principally on gateway modernisation and on the migration of issuer processing workloads to a containerised platform. The migration reduced authorisation latency at the ninety-fifth percentile from 118 milliseconds to 74 milliseconds.

## Item 8. Financial Statements and Supplementary Data

Net revenue was 3,418.2 million dollars in 2024, 3,062.8 million dollars in 2023 and 2,704.9 million dollars in 2022. Net income was 612.4 million dollars in 2024, 508.1 million dollars in 2023 and 402.6 million dollars in 2022. Total assets were 14,208.5 million dollars at December 31, 2024, including safeguarded client funds.
