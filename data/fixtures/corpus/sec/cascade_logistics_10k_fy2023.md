## Item 1. Business

Cascade Logistics Group, Inc. is an asset-light freight brokerage and contract logistics provider. At December 31, 2023 the carrier network comprised approximately 57,000 qualified carriers and the Company operated 38 contract warehouses totalling 16.1 million square feet. Cascade employed 15,600 people at December 31, 2023.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

Freight brokerage gross margins are compressed when spot capacity is abundant. Our Brokerage net revenue margin was 16.8 percent in 2023 compared with 19.4 percent in 2022.

We rely on third-party carriers. Cargo claims expense was 31.2 million dollars in 2023.

Fuel price volatility affects both purchased transportation cost and the fuel surcharge revenue we collect. A sustained divergence between the two would compress margin.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Total revenue for 2023 was 10,514.7 million dollars, a decrease of 13.2 percent from 12,108.3 million dollars in 2022, reflecting the normalisation of freight rates from the elevated levels of 2021 and 2022.

Net revenue was 1,882.1 million dollars. Operating income was 442.6 million dollars. Net income was 312.7 million dollars, or 3.29 dollars per diluted share. Free cash flow was 401.3 million dollars.
