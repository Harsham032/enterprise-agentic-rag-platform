## Item 2. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Revenue for the second quarter of fiscal 2025 was 587.3 million dollars, an increase of 8.4 percent from 541.8 million dollars in the second quarter of fiscal 2024. Data centre revenue grew 47 percent year over year and represented 24 percent of quarterly revenue.

Gross margin was 60.2 percent compared with 57.9 percent in the prior year quarter, reflecting improved factory loading and the release of 6.1 million dollars of previously reserved inventory that was subsequently sold.

Distributor weeks of inventory on hand declined to 9.6 weeks from 11.4 weeks at fiscal year end 2024, indicating that the destocking cycle described in our most recent annual report has largely run its course.

Research and development expense was 126.4 million dollars. Net income was 112.8 million dollars, or 0.93 dollars per diluted share.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

There have been no material changes to the risk factors disclosed in our annual report on Form 10-K for fiscal 2024, other than as set out below. In March 2025 our primary foundry partner announced a capacity reallocation affecting the 65 nanometre node used by several of our industrial product families. We currently expect to secure sufficient allocation for fiscal 2025 requirements, but a further reallocation would require qualification at an alternative foundry, a process we estimate would take between nine and fifteen months.
