## Item 1. Business

Vertexa Biopharma Corporation is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing targeted therapies for haematological malignancies and for rare metabolic disease. The Company's lead candidate, VXB-214, is an oral small molecule inhibitor in a pivotal Phase 3 trial for relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma. A second candidate, VXB-330, is in Phase 2 for a rare urea cycle disorder.

The Company has no approved products and has not generated product revenue. Collaboration revenue of 84.6 million dollars in 2024 was earned under a development and commercialisation agreement covering ex-North American rights to VXB-214.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

We have incurred significant operating losses since inception and expect to continue to do so. Accumulated deficit was 1,842.6 million dollars at December 31, 2024. We will require substantial additional capital to complete the development of VXB-214, and we may be unable to raise it on acceptable terms.

Our business depends heavily on the success of a single lead candidate. VXB-214 accounted for approximately 74 percent of research and development expenditure in 2024. A clinical hold, an unfavourable Phase 3 readout or a regulatory refusal to file would have a material adverse effect on our prospects and on the value of our securities.

We rely on a small number of contract manufacturers for drug substance. A single facility in Ireland produces all clinical supply of the VXB-214 active pharmaceutical ingredient. We have initiated technology transfer to a second site but do not expect it to be qualified before the second half of 2026.

Clinical trial enrolment is subject to competition. Four other sponsors are running pivotal trials in overlapping relapsed or refractory myeloma populations, and our enrolment rate in the fourth quarter of 2024 was 18 percent below plan.

Our intellectual property position is uncertain. The composition of matter patent covering VXB-214 expires in 2038 before any patent term extension, and we are aware of two third-party applications that, if granted as filed, could be asserted against our manufacturing process.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Collaboration revenue was 84.6 million dollars in 2024 compared with 42.0 million dollars in 2023, reflecting the achievement of a development milestone in the second quarter.

Research and development expense was 486.9 million dollars compared with 421.3 million dollars in 2023, driven by Phase 3 site activation costs and by the manufacture of registration-stage clinical supply. General and administrative expense was 118.2 million dollars.

Net loss was 498.7 million dollars, or 4.12 dollars per share, compared with a net loss of 462.8 million dollars, or 4.06 dollars per share, in 2023. Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were 1,206.4 million dollars at December 31, 2024. We believe our existing cash resources are sufficient to fund planned operations into the first half of 2027.

## Item 8. Financial Statements and Supplementary Data
