## Item 1. Business

Cascade Logistics Group, Inc. is an asset-light freight brokerage and contract logistics provider operating in North America and western Europe. The Company arranges truckload, less-than-truckload and intermodal transportation through a carrier network of approximately 61,000 qualified carriers, and operates 42 contract warehouses totalling 18.4 million square feet. Revenue is reported in two segments: Brokerage, which accounted for 68 percent of 2024 revenue, and Contract Logistics, which accounted for 32 percent.

The Company's ten largest customers represented 29 percent of 2024 revenue, and no single customer represented more than 6 percent. Cascade employed 14,900 people at December 31, 2024.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

Freight brokerage gross margins are compressed when spot truckload capacity is abundant. Average spot linehaul rates excluding fuel fell 11 percent during 2024, and our Brokerage net revenue margin declined to 14.1 percent from 16.8 percent in 2023. A further sustained decline in spot rates would reduce segment profitability.

We rely on third-party carriers whom we do not employ or control. Carrier insolvency, service failure or cargo loss can expose us to claims from shippers. Cargo claims expense was 38.4 million dollars in 2024 compared with 31.2 million dollars in 2023.

A substantial portion of our transaction volume depends on a single proprietary matching platform. An extended outage of that platform, whether caused by a security incident, a cloud provider disruption or a defect in our own software, would force manual load matching and would materially reduce daily transaction throughput. In November 2024 a regional cloud availability zone failure degraded platform performance for approximately nine hours; we estimate the associated revenue impact at 4.1 million dollars.

We are subject to worker classification litigation. Two putative class actions filed in 2023 allege that owner-operator drivers engaged through our dedicated fleet programme were misclassified as independent contractors. An adverse outcome could require reclassification and retroactive wage and benefit payments.

Our contract logistics business commits to fixed-price service levels under multi-year agreements. Warehouse labour costs rose 6.7 percent in 2024, and roughly 44 percent of contract logistics revenue sits under agreements without an annual labour indexation clause.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Total revenue for 2024 was 9,842.0 million dollars, a decrease of 6.4 percent from 10,514.7 million dollars in 2023. The decline reflects lower realised freight rates, partially offset by a 9 percent increase in loads brokered and by 380.2 million dollars of incremental contract logistics revenue from four new warehouse start-ups.

Net revenue, which the Company defines as total revenue less purchased transportation and warehouse occupancy costs, was 1,706.4 million dollars compared with 1,882.1 million dollars in 2023. Operating income was 318.9 million dollars compared with 442.6 million dollars in 2023, reflecting margin compression in Brokerage and 46.8 million dollars of start-up costs in Contract Logistics.

Net income was 201.4 million dollars, or 2.18 dollars per diluted share, compared with 312.7 million dollars, or 3.29 dollars per diluted share, in 2023. Free cash flow was 288.5 million dollars. The Company repurchased 3.1 million shares for 142.0 million dollars during 2024.

## Item 8. Financial Statements and Supplementary Data
