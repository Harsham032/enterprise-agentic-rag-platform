## Item 1. Business

Meridian Semiconductor, Inc. designs and sells mixed-signal and power management integrated circuits for industrial automation, automotive and data centre power delivery applications. The Company is fabless: all wafer production is outsourced to third-party foundries, and assembly and test are performed by outsourced partners in Malaysia, Taiwan and Vietnam.

Revenue by end market in fiscal 2024 was 41 percent industrial, 33 percent automotive, 19 percent data centre and 7 percent other. Distributors accounted for 62 percent of revenue. The Company held 1,840 issued patents at fiscal year end and employed 4,320 people, of whom 2,610 were engineers.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

We depend entirely on third-party foundries for wafer production, and a single foundry produced 71 percent of our wafer volume in fiscal 2024. We have no internal manufacturing capability and could not qualify an alternative source for our most advanced process nodes in less than twelve months. Capacity allocation decisions by that foundry, or a natural disaster affecting its facilities, would directly interrupt our ability to supply customers.

Our assembly and test operations are geographically concentrated. Approximately 68 percent of packaged unit volume passed through two facilities located within 40 kilometres of one another. We maintain approximately eleven weeks of finished-goods inventory as a partial mitigation.

Automotive design cycles are long and revenue recognition is back-loaded. A design win secured today typically enters volume production between three and five years later, so a lost socket has a delayed but durable revenue effect that is difficult to offset.

Export control regulations restrict the sale of certain power management devices to specified end users and jurisdictions. Changes to those regulations reduced our addressable revenue by an estimated 34 million dollars in fiscal 2024, and further tightening could affect additional product lines.

Our results are sensitive to distributor inventory behaviour. Distributor weeks of inventory on hand rose to 11.4 weeks at fiscal year end 2024 from 8.9 weeks a year earlier, and a distributor destocking cycle would reduce reported revenue ahead of any change in end demand.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Revenue for fiscal 2024 was 2,146.8 million dollars, a decrease of 9.1 percent from 2,361.9 million dollars in fiscal 2023. Industrial revenue declined 17 percent on distributor destocking, automotive revenue grew 6 percent, and data centre revenue grew 28 percent on power delivery content gains in accelerated computing platforms.

Gross margin was 58.4 percent compared with 62.1 percent in fiscal 2023. The 370 basis point decline reflects lower factory loading charges passed through by our foundry partners, an unfavourable mix shift toward lower-margin industrial parts, and 22.4 million dollars of excess inventory reserves.

Research and development expense was 486.2 million dollars, or 22.6 percent of revenue, compared with 452.7 million dollars, or 19.2 percent of revenue, in fiscal 2023. The Company maintained R&D spending through the downturn to protect its automotive design win pipeline.

Operating income was 421.0 million dollars compared with 631.8 million dollars. Net income was 372.4 million dollars, or 3.06 dollars per diluted share, compared with 548.9 million dollars, or 4.41 dollars per diluted share. Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments were 1,904.2 million dollars at fiscal year end.
